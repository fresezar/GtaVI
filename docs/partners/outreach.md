# Partner teklifi: şablonlar

İç belge. Hedef: 10 bin+ takipçili GTA hesapları (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X). Gönderen: kurucu.
Kural: mesajda yalnızca sitede çalışan şeyleri vaat et. `[KÖŞELİ]` alanları her hesap için doldur.
Gelir: reklam yokken rakam verme. Pay, partner linkinden gelen trafiğe göre, reklam açılınca konuşulur.

## 1. İlk mesaj

```
Hey [NAME], big fan of your GTA 6 posts. [ONE SPECIFIC POST OR DETAIL YOU LIKED].

I run Six Star (sixstargta.com), a community map for GTA 6 where players pin what they find, and every pin carries the finder's name.

I'd like to make you a founding partner. What you get:
- Your own page: sixstargta.com/p/[slug], with your name and logo. Your followers land there and add their finds in a few taps.
- Credit on every pin they add: "Found by @user · via [NAME]"
- A weekly Short on @SixStarGTAMap featuring your community's best finds, with your handle and link
- Monthly stats: how many people came from you and how many pins they added
- Revenue: no ads on the site yet. When they go live, partners get a share of the traffic they send, measured by that same link.

All I'd ask: one post or story pointing your followers to your page. No contract, no exclusivity.

Want me to set up your page so you can see it first?
```

## 2. "Evet" ya da "göster" derlerse

```
Done: sixstargta.com/p/[slug]
Open it on your phone and try adding a pin. It takes under a minute.

For the post, anything works. Example caption:
"Found something hidden in GTA 6? Put it on the map with your name on it 👉 link in bio"

I'll send you the first stats a week after your post.
```

## 3. Cevap yoksa (4-5 gün sonra, tek sefer)

```
Quick follow-up: I set up a page for you anyway, no strings: sixstargta.com/p/[slug]
If it's not for you, no worries. I'll keep enjoying your content either way.
```

## 4. "Ne kadar para?" derlerse

```
Honest answer: the site is new and there are no ads yet, so I won't make up a number.
What I can promise: when ads or subscriptions start, founding partners get a share of the revenue from the traffic they send, tracked by your link from day one. Your early traffic counts.
```

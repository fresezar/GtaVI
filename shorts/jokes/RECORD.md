# Kayıt kâğıdı

Her bölüm için metni **baştan sona tek seferde** oku ve `shorts/jokes/epNN/voice/take.m4a` (ya da .wav) olarak kaydet.
Sonra Claude'a "epNN'i senin sesinle yap" de: kayıt satırlara bölünür, video yeniden üretilir, telaffuz notu `accent-log.csv`'ye yazılır.

- Satırlar arasında 1 saniye dur. Bir cümleyi yanlış okursan bölümü baştan kaydet: araç aynı cümlenin iki okunuşundan hangisini alacağını bilemez.
- Telefonu ağzından bir karış uzak tut (önceki kayıtta tepe noktaları patlıyordu).
- Parantez içindekiler kimin konuştuğu ve nasıl; onları okuma.
- Vurucu son cümleden önce bir nefes dur.

## ep01: The Story of the Man With One Ball

- (anlatıcı) Where I'm from, we tell this one. There once was a man with the worst luck in the world.
- (anlatıcı) Oh, and he only had one ball.
- (anlatıcı) One day, he took a flight.
- (anlatıcı) Halfway there, the engine died.
- (kaptan) Someone has to jump!
- (anlatıcı) They drew a name. It was him.
- (adam) No way!
- (anlatıcı) They drew again. Him. Again. Him. Five times in a row!
- (anlatıcı) Finally, he said:
- (adam) Fine. One question. Get it right, and I'll jump.
- (adam) How many balls do this guy and I have, together?
- (herkes, gülerek) Ha ha ha! Four!
- (adam) Wrong!
- (anlatıcı) He showed his one ball.
- (anlatıcı) Then the guy across the aisle stood up,
- (anlatıcı) and showed his three.
- (anlatıcı) One plus three. Four.
- (anlatıcı) And so... he jumped.

## ep02: The Man Who Found a Penguin

- (anlatıcı) One morning, a man was walking down the street,
- (anlatıcı) when he found a penguin.
- (anlatıcı) Just a penguin. Standing there. Looking at him.
- (anlatıcı) A police officer walked by.
- (adam) Officer! What do I do with this penguin?
- (polis) Take him to the zoo!
- (anlatıcı) The next day, the officer saw them again.
- (anlatıcı) Same man. Same penguin. Holding hands. Eating ice cream.
- (polis) I told you to take him to the zoo!
- (adam) I did! He loved it.
- (adam) Today, we're going to the movies.

## ep03: The Man Who Bought a Parrot

- (anlatıcı) A man bought a parrot.
- (anlatıcı) Beautiful bird. Terrible mouth.
- (papağan) Bleep you! Bleep your bleeping shoes!
- (anlatıcı) He tried everything. Kindness.
- (adam) Please, be nice.
- (papağan) Bleep off!
- (anlatıcı) Finally, he lost it. He put the parrot in the freezer.
- (anlatıcı) One minute. Silence.
- (anlatıcı) He opened the door. The parrot stepped out.
- (papağan) I am deeply sorry for my language, sir. It will never happen again.
- (papağan) May I ask... what did the chicken do?

## ep04: The Man Who Thought He Was a Moth

- (anlatıcı) A man walked into a doctor's office.
- (adam) Doctor, I think I'm a moth.
- (anlatıcı) The doctor looked at him for a long time.
- (doktor) Sir, you don't need a doctor. You need a psychiatrist.
- (doktor) So why did you come in here?
- (adam) The light was on.

## ep05: The Man Who Bought a Talking Dog

- (anlatıcı) A man saw a sign. Talking dog for sale. Ten dollars.
- (anlatıcı) The old owner pointed to the backyard.
- (adam) Can you... talk?
- (köpek) Sure! I used to work for the CIA.
- (köpek) I caught spies in twelve countries.
- (köpek) Then I flew planes. Then I saved forty people from a fire.
- (köpek) Now I'm retired. I like the sun.
- (anlatıcı) The man ran back to the owner.
- (adam) That dog is amazing! Why only ten dollars?
- (yaşlı adam) Because he's a liar.
- (yaşlı adam) He never did any of that.

## ep06: The Man Who Hurt Everywhere

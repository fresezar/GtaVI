# Joke pool

Old jokes are folklore: we retell them in our own words and our own pictures. Ranked by how well they animate:
one clear setup, a visual punchline, under 45 s, no wordplay that needs a native accent.
Mark used ones with `→ epNN`. Sezar's own jokes go under "Own".

## Strongest (make first)

1. **The penguin and the zoo.** A man finds a penguin; a cop says "take him to the zoo". Next day the cop sees them
   again: "I told you to take him to the zoo!" "I did. He loved it. Today we're going to the movies." (~30 s) → ep02 (made 2026-11-21)
2. **The swearing parrot.** A man's parrot swears at everyone. He puts it in the freezer for a minute. The parrot
   comes out polite: "I'm sorry for my words... may I ask what the chicken did?" (~35 s) → ep03 (made 2026-11-21)
3. **"I think I'm a moth."** A man at the doctor's: "I think I'm a moth." "You need a psychiatrist, not a doctor.
   Why did you come in here?" "The light was on." (~20 s) → ep04
4. **Talking dog for sale.** The dog tells of spying for the CIA, saving lives, flying planes. "Why sell such a dog?"
   "Because he's a liar. He never did any of that." (~40 s, montage) → ep05
5. **Two boats and a helicopter.** Flood; a man refuses a boat, another boat, a helicopter: "God will save me." In
   heaven: "Why didn't you save me?" "I sent you two boats and a helicopter!" (~40 s)
6. **Three wishes on an island.** Three men, one genie. Two wish to go home. The third is lonely: "I wish my friends
   were back." (~35 s)
7. **It hurts everywhere.** "Doctor, it hurts when I touch my knee, my head, my belly..." "Your finger is broken."
   (~20 s)
8. **The snail and the turtles.** A snail is robbed by two turtles. Police: "What happened?" "I don't know. It all
   happened so fast." (~15 s)
9. **Keys under the streetlight.** A man looks for his keys under a lamp. "Did you lose them here?" "No, over there.
   But the light is better here." (~20 s; Nasreddin Hodja has the same story)
10. **The hearing aid.** An old man gets a new hearing aid. "Your family must love it!" "I haven't told them. I've
    changed my will three times." (~25 s)
